COMPANY SUPPORT POLICY
Returns and refunds: Customers may request a return within 30 days of delivery if the item is unused and in its original packaging. Approved refunds are normally processed within 5 to 10 business days after the returned item is received.
Shipping: Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 7 business days after dispatch. Delivery estimates may vary.
Support hours: Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM local time, excluding public holidays.
Order changes: Orders can only be changed or cancelled before dispatch.
Security: Never share passwords, full card numbers, or OTPs with support.
Escalation: If the knowledge base cannot answer, contact a human support representative. The assistant must not invent order status or account information.
